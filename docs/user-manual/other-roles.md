# Other roles

## Pharmacist
Menus: **Dashboard, Schedules, Stock**. **Stock** ("Medicines and supplies, the places they are kept, and what is on the shelf") lets a pharmacist view items, stores and stock on hand. Adding items, stores and opening stock, and approving stock, are admin-only today.

## Lab technician and viewer
Menus: **Dashboard** and **Schedules** only (read-only). There are no lab screens yet.

## Platform operator (MediCore team, not hospital staff)
The **Platform** menu shows **Hospitals** and **Hospital requests**, where new hospital sign-ups are reviewed and approved. Hospital staff never see it.

## New hospital sign-up (public)
At **/register-hospital**, a hospital fills **Hospital name**, **Your name**, **Work email**, **Phone**, **City** and **Hospital time zone** and presses **Send request**. Messages: "Thank you. Your request was received.", "This email is already registered. Please sign in or contact support." (error 409), "Too many registrations were sent from here. Please try again later."

## Patients (public pages)
Patients may get a payment link (**Your bill**) or a consent link (**Your choices** → **Save my choices**). An expired consent link says "This link has expired. Ask the hospital for a new one."
